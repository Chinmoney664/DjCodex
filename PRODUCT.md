# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People discovering DJ Codex through social media who want to quickly see upcoming shows, event details, ticket links, and any available promo code.

## Product Purpose

Present Codex as a New York DJ project and make the next show easy to understand and attend. Success means visitors can identify the next event and reach its ticket page with minimal effort.

## Brand Personality

Nocturnal, polished, energetic. The experience should feel like a restrained club poster brought to life rather than a colorful generic link page.

## Anti-references

Avoid bright rainbow palettes, generic or crowded Linktree look-alikes, marketing-heavy landing pages, and decorative effects that compete with event information.

## Layout

Mobile is a link-in-bio page: centered logo, upcoming shows as compact bars (thumbnail, title, date, Tickets button with promo code under it), link buttons, then social/email icons. Desktop will get a separate design later.

## Design Principles

- Make the next event the clearest action.
- Let artist and event photography carry the atmosphere.
- Keep details concise and scannable on phones.
- Use motion sparingly and preserve the dark Codex identity.
- Keep event updates simple enough to maintain in one data file.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, useful image alt text, keyboard-accessible links, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support for animated effects.
